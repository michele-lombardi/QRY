# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TypePulse should be a welcoming project. Contributors and maintainers are
expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, accept constructive
feedback and make space for people with different backgrounds and experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing
private information, sustained disruption and sexualized or demeaning conduct.

## Scope

This standard applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and
other public spaces used to represent the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
